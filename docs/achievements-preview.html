Case Badges

Uptime Medals

Data centres and sysadmins celebrate uptime: 99.9%, then 99.99%, then five nines. Every award is a struck medal with a spun face and the icon engraved, and a set of four fills a 1U rack plate like the ones that hold the real servers.

Benchmark and overclocking leaderboards put the best machines in a Hall of Fame. Every award is a standing plaque with the details etched under a mirror pocket, and a set of four becomes a leaderboard frame on your wall.
